Thank You for Your Support

Sharp Hunter has been active since 2024. It started as a NEON Labs beta ahead of the NFL season and became a daily stop for bettors who wanted one thing: to bet with the sharp money. We’ve made the decision to wind Sharp Hunter down.

The idea was simple. Track the real-time bets of sharps alongside their historical performance, give every bet a Sharp Score™, and help bettors make more informed decisions. To everyone who used the app, read Sharp Hunter Daily, tested the beta and told us when we got one wrong: you made the product better.

Sharp Hunter was built by the team at NEON Labs, part of TruMedia Networks, our parent company. Their work in sports data continues, as does the work of our sister company, Poker Now (Rio Gaming). We hope you’ll check them out.

Thank you for hunting with us.

— The Sharp Hunter team
